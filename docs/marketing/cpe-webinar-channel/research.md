**Status:** Findings doc · 2026-07-06 · marketing/growth workstream
**Pairs with:** [`webinar-kit.md`](./webinar-kit.md) (the produced webinar assets)
**Method:** Web research (WebSearch/WebFetch), 2026-07-06. Anything we couldn't confirm is in a **FLAG** line. No contacts, prices, or program details were invented.

> **Why this doc exists.** CPAs carry mandatory annual CPE hours and trust their state societies far more than ads. A genuinely educational webinar hosted under or alongside a society (or a CPE platform that already holds the credential) is lead-gen and a durable content asset at once — the highest-trust channel for this audience. This doc figures out the credit mechanics, maps the accessible hosts, and picks the first targets to actually pursue.

## Part 1 · Summary of findings (read this first)

1. **You do NOT need your own NASBA sponsorship to give CPAs credit.** The fast, low-risk path is to **co-host under an existing approved sponsor** — a CPE marketplace (CPA Academy, myCPE) or a state society / association that already carries the NASBA registration and owns CPE compliance. Getting our own NASBA National Registry membership is a later move, once webinars are a proven, recurring channel.
2. **There is a timing wrinkle right now:** NASBA stopped accepting _new_ sponsor applications after **June 30, 2026** and **reopens August 1, 2026** under the 2026 Standards ([source](https://nasba.org/blog/2023/08/03/what-does-it-take-to-become-a-cpe-sponsor/)). So even if we wanted our own registration today, we can't apply until August — another reason v1 rides on someone else's credential.
3. **A live webinar needs no final exam.** The credit-bearing live format ("Group Internet Based") requires 50-min-per-credit timing, measurable learning objectives, real-time attendance monitoring (≥3 polls per credit hour), and an evaluation form — the _sponsor_ handles all of it. A graded final exam is only for self-study, which we are not doing.
4. **Several state societies sell a productized "sponsored webinar / CPE showcase"** where a vendor delivers a 50-minute CPE-qualifying session to members — notably **MICPA** (Learning Showcase, published dates, strictly educational) and **MNCPA** (Sponsored Webinar). These are the cleanest association co-host slots.
5. **The single biggest constraint is content posture:** every credit-bearing or society-hosted slot expects _education, not a demo_. MICPA states it plainly — "promotional or sales-focused content is not permitted." This maps exactly to our 80/20 rule in the [webinar kit](./webinar-kit.md): teach the problem, show the tool briefly and honestly at the end.
6. **Recommendation:** v0 = a non-credit educational webinar we can run in weeks; v1 = co-host a CPE-bearing session under a marketplace (myCPE / CPA Academy) or a society CPE product (MICPA / MNCPA / FICPA); v2 (later) = our own NASBA registration if the channel proves out. See [§4](#4--ranked-first-targets) for the exact first moves.

## 1 · NASBA / CPE mechanics — can we offer credit, and how?

### What "offering CPE" actually requires

The recognized credential is membership on the **NASBA National Registry of CPE Sponsors** — the list of organizations whose CPE state boards will accept ([nasbaregistry.org](https://www.nasbaregistry.org/), [what sponsors need to know](https://www.nasbaregistry.org/resources/what-sponsors-need-to-know)). Approval is gated by a review against the AICPA/NASBA _Statement on Standards for CPE Programs_, a signed compliance agreement, and ongoing obligations:

- **Record retention ≥ 5 years:** participation records, program dates, instructor/developer credentials, credits earned, and evaluation results (Standard No. 24).
- **Subject-matter-expert development + technical-accuracy review** of each program.
- **A program evaluation** collected and retained for every session.
- **Attendance monitoring** for real-time online programs (see below).
- **Annual renewal** and continued Standards compliance.

**Eligibility to apply:** the org must have "offered at least one educational program in the past" — it need not have been CPA-focused ([source](https://nasba.org/blog/2023/08/03/what-does-it-take-to-become-a-cpe-sponsor/)).

### Cost (verified where possible)

Annual renewal fees scale by number of distinct programs ([NASBA renewal schedule](https://www.nasbaregistry.org/current-sponsors/annual-renewal)):

| Programs | Annual renewal |
| -------- | -------------- |
| 1–15     | **$910**       |
| 16–25    | $1,128         |
| 26–50    | $1,820         |
| 51–100   | $2,907         |
| 101–250  | $3,588         |

- Initial application fee for **Nano Learning is $1,222** (verified). The **Group Internet Based (live webinar) initial fee is program-count-based but the exact dollar figure could not be confirmed** — the fee page 404'd repeatedly. **FLAG:** budget it as "roughly a first-year outlay in the ~$900–$1,200+ range, confirm with NASBA" (`cpe@nasba.org` / 1-866-627-2286). This range is an _estimate, not a sourced number_.
- Self-study (QAS) is a separate, costlier method we do **not** need for live webinars.

### Lead time

NASBA publishes no guaranteed end-to-end timeline. Confirmed sub-timers: interest form → application link in "2–3 days"; a 2-week window to respond to requested modifications; applications reviewed in order received. **FLAG (estimate):** industry practice suggests **~1–3 months** application-to-approval, longer if a course needs revision — plus the **July 2026 application blackout** pushes any new application to **Aug 1, 2026** at the earliest.

### Requirements for one credit-bearing LIVE webinar (Group Internet Based)

Grounded in the [AICPA/NASBA Standards](https://www.nasba.org/files/2012/02/AICPA_NASBAStandardsFinal.pdf) and [GIB measurement guidance](https://www.nasbaregistry.org/resources/best-practices/group-internet-based-measurement):

- **1 CPE credit = 50 minutes** of program (half-credits typically allowed after the first full credit).
- **Measurable learning objectives.**
- **Attendance monitoring — the defining requirement:** ≥ **3 polling/interaction instances per credit hour**, unpredictable enough to prove engagement.
- **No final exam** for live programs — attendance monitoring is the completion evidence.
- **An evaluation form** collected and retained.

(Contrast: self-study requires a ≥70% graded exam with ≥5 scored questions per credit — not our path.)

### State-specific wrinkle

Being on the NASBA Registry is accepted by most states, but some boards require **separate direct registration** — most-cited hard cases: **Florida, Hawaii, Ohio, Texas, New York** (also CA, DE, IL, LA, MS, OR, PA, SC, WA, WY per [cpaselfstudy.com/accreditation](https://www.cpaselfstudy.com/accreditation/) and [cpeonline.com/sponsor-information](https://cpeonline.com/sponsor-information)). **FLAG:** these lists come from CPE-vendor pages, not primary statute; verify against each state board before promising credit there. **This is another reason to let a sponsor who already carries multi-state acceptance own the credit.**

### Can a vendor co-present under an existing sponsor? (Yes — the recommended path)

Standard, well-trodden, and it means **no NASBA registration of our own.** The sponsor holds the registration and owns compliance (objectives, polling, attendance, records, credit issuance); we supply the topic + expert presenter (+ usually pay for the slot / receive the leads). Documented models:

- **CPA Academy** (NASBA sponsor, ID **#111889** seen in listings) — outside firms present free educational CPE webinars; platform issues credit, we get exposure + leads ([about](https://www.cpaacademy.org/s/about-us)).
- **Earmark** — turns a webinar/podcast into an audio CPE course; free to create ([authors](https://earmarkcpe.com/authors/)).
- **Real vendor precedent — Harbor Compliance × Earmark:** a software vendor made its monthly webinars CPE-bearing _through Earmark_, with no NASBA registration of its own ([blog](https://www.harborcompliance.com/blog/harbor-compliance-partners-with-earmark-cpe-to-offer-cpe-credit-for-monthly-webinars/)).
- **State societies** are themselves NASBA sponsors and co-host vendor/member webinars (see §2).

### Recommendation — the credit path

**Start co-hosted (or non-credit), defer our own registration.**

- **v0 (weeks):** non-credit educational webinar. Zero CPE machinery, validates the topic and audience. Landing page says "non-credit educational session," not "CPE."
- **v1 (~1 month to first credit-bearing run):** co-host under CPA Academy / myCPE **or** a society CPE product (MICPA / MNCPA / FICPA). Sponsor owns compliance and multi-state acceptance; we own content + presenter.
- **v2 (later, only if the channel proves out):** apply for our own NASBA GIB registration after Aug 1, 2026 (~$910/yr renewal + unconfirmed initial fee, ~1–3 mo runway, plus separate filings for NY/TX/FL/OH/HI).

## 4 · Ranked first targets
