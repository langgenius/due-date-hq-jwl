## 加州 · PTE 选择性税延长到 2030(SB 132)

**小红书标题**:`加州 PTE 税延到 2030!SALT 绕道续命 📅`（备选:`有加州 PTE 客户的会计师注意⚠️规则变了`）

**小红书配文**

```
加州 PTE 选择性税再延 5 年!📅 SB 132 把 SALT 上限绕道的选择性税延长到 2030 税年(原本 2025 到期)。有加州 PTE 客户的会计师,2026 起规则有变,先收藏。

【前情提要】
加州的 PTE 选择性税(俗称 SALT 绕道),让合伙企业 / S 公司股东绕开联邦州税抵扣上限。原定 2025 税年到期;2025 年 6 月 SB 132 签署,延长到 2030 税年。

【2026 起的变化】
· 可选年限:2025 → 2030 税年
· 6月15日 预缴:迟缴或少缴,不再取消当年选择资格(旧规则会直接取消,一分都不能差)
· 代价:迟缴/少缴的,该股东 PTE 抵免按其应缴未缴部分的 12.5% 削减
· FTB 已更新 2026 版 Form 3804 / 3893 / 3804-CR

来源:加州 FTB · SB 132

**LinkedIn 配文**

```
California just extended its PTE elective tax — the SALT-cap workaround — through 2030.

SB 132 (signed June 2025) keeps California's pass-through entity elective tax available for tax years 2026 through 2030; it had been set to expire after 2025.

What's new for 2026 onward:
• June 15 prepayment: a late or underpaid June 15 prepayment no longer disqualifies the election (under the old rules, missing it did).
• The trade-off: the owner's PTE credit is reduced by 12.5% of that owner's share of the amount due but unpaid by June 15.
• The FTB has updated Forms 3804, 3893, and 3804-CR for 2026.

If you have California PTE clients, the election survives — but the 2026 prepayment mechanics changed.

Source: California FTB — SB 132.
