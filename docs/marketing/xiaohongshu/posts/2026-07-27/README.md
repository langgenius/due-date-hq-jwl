## ① 北卡 · Mecklenburg 销售税 7.25%→8.25%(7/1 生效)

**小红书配文**
```
🟡 规则变更播报 · 北卡罗来纳州

梅克伦堡县(夏洛特所在县)销售与使用税率上调:7.25% → 8.25%,2026 年 7月1日起生效。

【前情提要】
2025 年 11 月 4 日全民公投通过,梅克伦堡县新增 1% 地方销售与使用税,用于道路与公共交通。这是夏洛特 28 年来首次销售税上调,合并税率从 7.25% 升至 8.25%。

【对申报的影响】
· 表格:2026 年 7月1日起(含)的税期,须用新版 E-500 / E-500E;新版预计 8月1日 由 NCDOR 发布
· 适用:多数应税商品(服装、电子、堂食、酒类)适用新增 1%;符合条件食品仍按 2%;电力、天然气、电信维持各自 7%
· 不替代现有 0.5% 交通税 —— 是叠加,不是替换
· 按销售归属地(sourcing)判断:归属到梅克伦堡县的销售适用新税率

【实务提示】
在梅克伦堡县有销售归属的零售商,7/1 前更新 POS/税率表,确认 8 月起用新表申报。6月30日 及之前的税期不变。

来源:NCDOR 重要通知「Mecklenburg County Sales and Use Tax Increase」

#美国报税 #CPA #注册会计师 #销售税 #北卡罗来纳 #夏洛特 #税务合规 #EA
```

**LinkedIn 配文**
```
North Carolina sales tax alert — Mecklenburg County.

Effective July 1, 2026, Mecklenburg County (which includes Charlotte) adds a 1% local sales & use tax, raising the combined rate from 7.25% to 8.25%. Voters approved the measure on November 4, 2025 to fund roads and transit — Charlotte's first sales tax increase in 28 years.

What practitioners and retailers need to know:
• New returns: use the updated Forms E-500 / E-500E for periods beginning on or after July 1, 2026. NCDOR expects them to be available August 1, 2026.
• Scope: the additional 1% applies to most taxable goods (clothing, electronics, dining out, alcohol). Qualifying food stays at 2%; electricity, piped natural gas, video programming, and telecommunications keep their 7% rates. It does not replace the existing 0.50% transit tax.
• Sourcing: the new rate applies to sales sourced to Mecklenburg County. Update POS and tax tables before July 1; periods ending on/before June 30, 2026 are unchanged.

Source: NCDOR Important Notice — Mecklenburg County Sales and Use Tax Increase.
```

## ② 佐治亚 · 东南部野火,联邦报税延至 8/20(GA-2026-03)

**小红书配文**
```
🔴 IRS 延期播报 · 佐治亚

因东南部野火,IRS 把佐治亚 3 个县的联邦报税与缴款截止日,统一延后到 8月20日。距新截止日还剩 24 天。

【前情提要】
2026 年 4 月 18 日起,佐治亚东南部发生野火及直线风灾。IRS 据此发布灾害减免(公告 GA-2026-03):受灾期内原本到期的联邦申报与缴税,统一延至 2026 年 8月20日。

【覆盖范围】
· 受灾县:Clinch、Echols、Brantley 三县
· 延期项:第二季度预缴(原 6月15日)、季度工资税 941(原 4月30 / 7月31日)、免税组织 990(原 5月15日),以及受灾期内到期的其它联邦申报与缴款
· 适用方式:地址在这三个县内的自动适用,无需申请;客户在县外、但账册或记账人在县内的,需主动致电 IRS 灾害热线单独申请

⚠️ 注意:4月15日的个人报税截止日在灾害发生(4/18)之前,不在本次延期范围。

来源:IRS 灾害减免公告 GA-2026-03(irs.gov 可查)
```

**LinkedIn 配文**
```
IRS disaster relief update — Southeast Georgia.

Following the wildfires and straight-line winds that began April 18, 2026, the IRS has postponed federal filing and payment deadlines to August 20, 2026 for taxpayers in three Georgia counties: Clinch, Echols, and Brantley.

What's covered — any federal return or payment originally due on or after April 18 and before August 20, including:
• Q2 estimated income tax (originally due June 15)
• Quarterly payroll returns, Form 941 (due April 30 and July 31)
• Calendar-year exempt-organization returns, Form 990 (due May 15)

Two things practitioners often miss:
• Relief is automatic if the taxpayer's address of record is in a covered county — no application needed. If your client is outside the area but their records or preparer are inside it, relief is NOT automatic; call the IRS disaster hotline.
• The April 15 individual filing deadline fell before the April 18 incident, so it is not postponed.

Source: IRS notice GA-2026-03.
```

```
IRS disaster relief — Washington State.

Following severe storms, straight-line winds, flooding, landslides and mudslides that began December 9, 2025, the IRS has postponed federal filing and payment deadlines to August 5, 2026 for taxpayers in 24 Washington counties (plus the Samish Indian Nation) — about nine days out.

What's covered — any federal return or payment originally due on or after December 9, 2025 and before August 5, 2026: individual (1040), corporate (1120 / 1120-S), partnership (1065), estate & trust (1041), tax-exempt (990), payroll and excise (941 / 940), quarterly estimated payments, and IRA / HSA contributions.
```
